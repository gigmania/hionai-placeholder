Opt-In Flow

Users opt in publicly by visiting the dedicated enrollment form on our website at https://tysonium.com/sms.html. The user manually enters their mobile number into the input field and must check an un-ticked consent box stating: "I agree to receive automated operational IT status alerts from Tysonium at this number. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help." No mobile numbers are ever pre-populated or registered without this explicit, verifiable web consent.
