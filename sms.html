SMS Notifications

Personal automation alerts by SMS.

HI on AI SMS notifications are used by the system owner to receive task-completion alerts from automation tools and coding assistants, then reply with follow-up instructions when needed.

Message Purpose

Messages are used for personal productivity and automation workflows, including alerts that a software agent has completed a task, needs input, or is ready for the next prompt.

Opt-In Flow

The recipient opts in by configuring their own mobile phone number in a private automation setup controlled by the system owner. The setup explains that SMS notifications will be sent for task status updates, that replies may be used as follow-up prompts, message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and STOP and HELP are supported.

SMS Terms

  • Message frequency varies based on task activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of future SMS notifications.
  • Reply HELP for help or contact tyson@tysonium.com.
  • Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Example Messages

HI on AI: Your coding assistant finished its task. Reply with the next instruction or STOP to opt out.

HI on AI: Task complete. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe.

HI on AI: Your agent needs input before continuing. Reply with instructions or STOP to opt out.
